Our story

Nwoke’m means “my man” in Igbo. It’s what you call the man who matters most, and it’s why we exist.

Why we started

Men are often the hardest people to shop for. Not because they don’t deserve thoughtful gifts, but because the usual options feel predictable, and putting together something special takes time most of us don’t have.

Nwoke’m was created to change that. We bring together useful luxury, personal touches and standout pieces, including our signature jacket, in boxes that feel like a gift before they’re even opened.

How we curate

Every item earns its place. If he wouldn’t use it, wear it or keep it, it doesn’t go in the box. Then we wrap it the way it deserves: rigid cream box, sage tissue, a gold seal and your words on the card.

Because every man deserves to feel seen, celebrated and appreciated.
