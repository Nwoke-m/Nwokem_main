Help

Delivery, payment, sizing and everything else you might want to know before you order.

Delivery

Delivery within Lagos in 1–3 working days. Nationwide delivery available on request.

Want it to arrive on a specific day? Tell us the date when you order and we’ll plan around it.

Payment

Place your order on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm your box, delivery fee and payment details. Your order is confirmed once payment is received.

Questions

How do I order?

Choose a box and tap the order button. It opens WhatsApp with your order ready to send, and we’ll take it from there.

Can I add a personal note?

Yes. Every box can include a handwritten note in your words at no extra cost.

Can I change what’s inside a box?

Our Essential and Signature boxes are curated sets. If you’d like something different, the Bespoke Box is built entirely around him.

How far ahead should I order?

For Essential and Signature boxes, a few days is usually enough. Please order Bespoke boxes at least 7 days ahead. Busy seasons like Valentine’s and Christmas book up early.

Do you do corporate orders?

Yes. Visit our corporate gifts page to request a quote.

What if the jacket doesn’t fit?

Message us on WhatsApp within a few days of delivery and we’ll help you arrange an exchange, subject to availability.

Tell us who he is and what the occasion is. We’ll help you choose the right box.
